= Littelfuse MIDI HP 70 V fuse integration

== Purpose

This slice makes a real DC fuse an explicit EMES power-path component and consumes its manufacturer ratings in executable design checks.

The selected reviewed part is Littelfuse `4998040.M-M6`, a 40 A MIDI High Performance Series bolt-down fuse with a 70 V DC rating. The reviewed manufacturer datasheet also records a 2.5 kA interrupting rating at 70 V DC, typical 94 mV voltage drop, 1.56 mOhm cold resistance, 4700 A^2*s I^2t, and a 38 A typical maximum-allowed-current point at 20 degC.

The normalized source record is `catalogs/sources/littelfuse-midi70v-40a.source.json`; the deterministic catalog projection is `catalogs/littelfuse-midi70v-40a.catalog.json`.

== Executable semantics

`org.emes.power.power_paths[].fuse_component` identifies an optional catalog-backed series fuse. The fused P45B example uses:

[source,text]
----
battery pack -> main fuse -> DC/DC converter -> load
----

`tools/battery_known_envelope.py` consumes the fuse in two ways:

* `rated_voltage_dc` must be at least the maximum pack voltage. The 70 V fuse therefore has 28 V margin over the 42 V maximum 10S P45B pack voltage. An otherwise identical 30 V fuse is rejected.
* `rated_current` is converted to a dimensionless load-scale factor against modeled battery-side current. The 40 A fuse has a 1.9x factor at the 500 W reference load when compared with the maximum pack-side current over the modeled battery voltage range. The manufacturer-backed SynQor NQ60 input-current limit and the nominal 40 A fuse are both 1.9x at the reference point; an exact 20 degC request adds the source-backed 38 A fuse candidate at 1.805x, while a 15 A counterexample moves the weakest-link identity to the fuse at 0.7125x.

The fuse rating is not coerced into an invented common wattage rating. Heterogeneous limits remain in their native engineering domains and are compared only through dimensionless reference-load scale factors.

== Deliberate limits

The 40 A nominal rating is used as a conservative known-limit cap. It is not claimed to be a 10-second pulse rating.

The source-conditioned 38 A value at 20 degC is preserved in the catalog but is not silently applied at other temperatures. The time-current curve and I^2t value are likewise retained as source facts; this slice does not infer an arbitrary 10-second ampacity from them.

The Littelfuse manufacturer PDF is reviewable through its upstream URL, but GitHub-hosted runners receive HTTP 403 when attempting raw PDF acquisition. The authorized remote-workstation connector was also unavailable during this slice. Therefore no PDF byte hash is fabricated: the source snapshot explicitly records `raw_bytes_not_pinned`, and deterministic CI verifies the reviewed snapshot-to-catalog projection instead.

Raw-byte pinning remains follow-up work. Until the broader battery safety evidence is complete, this analysis is sizing/traceability evidence only and is not approval to fabricate, charge, or energize a battery pack.
