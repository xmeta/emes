= Weakest-Known Battery-Path Envelope

== Purpose

`tools/battery_known_envelope.py` combines the source-conditioned cell pulse envelope with downstream limits that EMES can currently prove from selected catalog parts.

The key rule is to preserve each rating in its native engineering domain. Cell pulse power is compared as power, BMS capability as pack-side current, converter capability as output current, and fuse capability as pack-side current plus a separate DC-voltage compatibility check. For fixed output power, pack-side current limits are evaluated against the maximum demand over the modeled battery voltage range (the 25 V minimum for the 10S P45B example), not only at nominal pack voltage. EMES does not manufacture a common power rating by multiplying every current limit by an arbitrary voltage.

== Dimensionless load scaling

For each known limit, the evaluator computes a dimensionless scale factor:

[stem]
+++++
s_i = \frac{limit_i}{reference\ demand_i}
+++++

The known-component load-scale limit is:

[stem]
+++++
s_{known} = \min_i s_i
+++++

This permits comparison of heterogeneous constraints without erasing their native units or rating semantics.

== Base reference result

For the P45B 10S2P example at 10 s and 50% SOC:

* cell pulse envelope scale: 6.384;
* BMS continuous-current scale: 2.85;
* converter continuous-output-current scale: 2.88;
* converter continuous-input-current scale: 1.9.

The 500 W load requires approximately 14.62 A at the 36 V nominal pack point, but approximately 21.05 A at the modeled 25 V pack minimum with the explicit 95% EMES converter-efficiency assumption. The real JKBMS 60 A continuous rating therefore has a 2.85 load scale. The SynQor NQ60 40 A output-current ceiling has a 2.88 scale, while its 40 A input-current limit is tighter at 1.9. The known-component output envelope is therefore 950 W and the corresponding reference input envelope is 1000 W.

The JKBMS and SynQor current limits are manufacturer-backed. The 95% efficiency used for the reference calculation is separately tagged as an EMES analysis assumption. Continuous ratings are used as conservative known caps in the 10-second comparison and are not relabeled as pulse ratings.

== SynQor input-damping prerequisite

The NQ60 manufacturer snapshot also preserves the recommended-operating-condition requirement for at least 1500 uF of external input capacitance with ESR strictly greater than 50 mOhm. Each current NQ60 power path therefore declares a requirement-level input-damping network of 1500 uF, 60 mOhm, and 63 V. The power evaluator requires capacitance at or above the source-backed minimum, ESR strictly above the source-backed threshold, and voltage rating at or above the full modeled 42 V pack maximum. Missing damping declarations fail closed.

This declaration is intentionally not a selected physical capacitor or fabricated network. Satisfying the datasheet minimums is necessary but does not prove source/converter small-signal stability over line, load, and temperature. Evidence therefore records `check_scope=datasheet_minimums_only` and `system_stability_verified=false`; detailed stability analysis, pre-charge/inrush validation, EMI behavior, and fabrication approval remain separate work.

== Explicit fuse variant

`examples/power-pack-molicel-p45b-fused/mechanism.json` adds a catalog-backed Littelfuse MIDI High Performance 70 V `4998040.M-M6` fuse in series between the battery pack and converter.

The nominal 40 A fuse rating is a separate weakest-link candidate. For the 10S P45B pack, the maximum pack voltage is 42 V, so the 70 Vdc fuse rating has a 28 V compatibility margin. At the 500 W reference load the nominal fuse-current load scale is 1.9 against the maximum modeled pack-side current, tying the SynQor input-current limit; the nominal-only output envelope is 950 W.

The nominal current rating is not treated as a fuse time-current model. EMES does not infer a 10-second fuse ampacity from nominal current, I2t, or sparse time-current data.

== Exact source-conditioned ambient derating

The reviewed Littelfuse catalog snapshot also contains a distinct manufacturer point `max_allowed_current_20c = 38 A`, conditioned on `ambient_temperature == 20 degC`.

This value is deliberately not flattened into the 40 A nominal rating. It is consumed only when a known-envelope analysis request explicitly supplies the matching context, for example:

[source,json]
----
{
  "analysis_id": "A_KNOWN_ENVELOPE_FUSE_20C",
  "path_id": "PATH_MAIN",
  "fuse_current_properties": ["max_allowed_current_20c"],
  "context": {
    "ambient_temperature": {"value": 20.0, "unit": "degC"}
  }
}
----

At exactly 20 degC, the evaluator keeps both candidates:

* nominal fuse current: 40 A, load scale 1.9;
* source-conditioned fuse current: 38 A, load scale 1.805.

The 38 A point is therefore more restrictive than both the nominal fuse rating and the SynQor input-current limit, producing a 1.805 load scale and a 902.5 W output envelope for the exact 20 degC context.

If an analysis request explicitly supplies an unsupported ambient temperature, EMES rejects the analysis rather than interpolating, extrapolating, or silently falling back to the nominal fuse current. Omitting a known-envelope analysis request preserves the previous nominal-only behavior and records that ambient derating was not applied.

== Executable counterexamples

Focused CI verifies several limiting-identity and fail-closed cases:

* the base reference asserts both the 14.62 A nominal-point current and the 21.05 A maximum current over the modeled pack-voltage range, and verifies that using the latter makes the 30 A BMS the limiting known component;
* reducing the fuse nominal rating to 15 A moves the fused-example minimum to the nominal fuse-current candidate;
* keeping the nominal fuse rating at 40 A but changing the exact 20 degC source-conditioned point to 15 A moves the minimum to the source-conditioned fuse-current candidate;
* reducing fuse DC voltage rating to 30 V is rejected against the 42 V maximum pack voltage;
* requesting 21 degC when only the exact 20 degC source-conditioned fuse-current point is available is rejected rather than interpolated or nominally substituted;
* altering the recorded parent power-evidence digest in pulse evidence is rejected.

These checks verify that limiting-element identity and source-condition applicability are computed from evidence rather than hard-coded.

== Remaining boundary

This is intentionally called the _weakest-known-component envelope_, not a complete system pulse rating.

Cell interconnect, bus conductor, contactor, temperature rise beyond the explicitly supported fuse ambient point, cell imbalance, aging, enclosure, and other applicable limits are not represented in this calculation. Connector limits are composed in the separate downstream connector-envelope stage. Adding any remaining applicable limit may only maintain or reduce the known envelope.

The reviewed Littelfuse snapshot is reproducible from the committed reviewed source snapshot, but the upstream PDF raw-byte digest is not yet pinned because automated source acquisition is blocked. This prevents treating the current result as physical safety approval.

Physical fabrication, charging, and energization remain outside automatic approval.
