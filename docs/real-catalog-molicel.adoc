= Authoritative Molicel INR-21700-P45B Catalog

== Purpose

This catalog slice connects an attributable manufacturer cell snapshot to EMES power sizing while preserving a strict boundary between executable rating checks and physical battery safety approval.

The snapshot authority is Molicel Product Data Sheet v1.4 for `INR-21700-P45B`:

* upstream: `https://www.molicel.com/wp-content/uploads/INR21700P45B_1.4_Product-Data-Sheet-of-INR-21700-P45B-80109.pdf`
* pinned bytes: `400272`
* SHA-256: `54832eb1684991f097578f02dd9ecfc3ba49cbc8bea40beacd7103092b717e69`

The PDF is referenced and content-addressed but is not redistributed by this repository.

== Normalized properties

The manufacturer snapshot records, among other values:

* nominal voltage: 3.6 V;
* charge voltage: 4.2 V;
* discharge voltage: 2.5 V;
* typical capacity: 4.5 Ah / 16.2 Wh;
* minimum capacity: 4.3 Ah / 15.5 Wh;
* standard charge current: 4.5 A;
* maximum charge current: 13.5 A with a 70 degC cut-off;
* continuous discharge current: 45 A with an 80 degC cut-off;
* charge temperature range: 0 degC to 60 degC;
* discharge temperature range: -40 degC to 60 degC;
* typical AC impedance: 7 mOhm at 30% SOC;
* typical DC impedance: 15 mOhm at 50% SOC;
* maximum diameter: 21.55 mm;
* maximum height: 70.15 mm;
* maximum mass: 70 g.

== Conditional-rating invariant

A rating and the conditions under which the manufacturer states that rating are one engineering fact.

The reviewed source snapshot therefore keeps the literal manufacturer facts such as `cutoff_temperature == 80 degC` for continuous discharge and `cutoff_temperature == 70 degC` for maximum charge.

The Molicel provider converts those literal cut-off facts into conservative consumer contracts that can be checked against a selected protection component:

[source,text]
----
continuous_discharge_current = 45 A
requires cell_discharge_cutoff_temperature <= 80 degC

maximum_charge_current = 13.5 A
requires cell_charge_cutoff_temperature <= 70 degC
----

The SOC conditions on impedance values remain exact measurement conditions rather than protection-control contracts.

Normal CI asserts deterministic regeneration of the catalog and exact preservation of these normalized conditions.

== Executable discharge-rating control

`examples/power-pack-molicel-p45b/mechanism.json` binds the authoritative P45B cell to the hash-pinned JKBMS `JK-BD6A20S-6P` catalog part and a synthetic DC/DC fixture. The JKBMS manual states that discharge over-temperature protection is user-configurable and gives 70 degC as the Li-ion factory default. EMES therefore does not flatten 70 degC into an immutable hardware property.

When `tools/power.py` consumes the P45B `continuous_discharge_current` property it resolves the cell condition through an explicit mechanism binding from `P_BMS_DISCHARGE_OVERTEMP` to the JKBMS source-backed `default_cell_discharge_cutoff_temperature`. The evaluator records the rated property, condition, controlling component/property, selected design parameter, control value, and pass status in `rating_condition_results`.

The current mechanism fixes that setting at the only source-backed value used by this slice, 70 degC, which satisfies the normalized P45B `<= 80 degC` contract. The 10S2P cell-side 90 A raw capability is clamped by the real JKBMS 60 A continuous-discharge rating before pack capability is reported.

CI also creates negative variants:

* selecting 75 degC is rejected because this slice has no source-backed configuration evidence for that selected value;
* removing the explicit condition binding is rejected rather than guessing a control;
* 6S is rejected by the JKBMS 7S Li-ion minimum, and 7S is rejected because the P45B minimum pack voltage is 17.5 V, below the BMS 20 V supply minimum.

The maximum-charge rating is preserved with its own 70 degC control condition but is not consumed by the current discharge-only power path because no charger analysis is implemented yet.

== Safety and fidelity boundary

Passing this analytic check means only that the selected data and explicit protection-control condition are internally consistent for the implemented sizing model.

It does not establish cell temperature prediction, ambient-temperature suitability, voltage sag, state-of-charge behavior, cell balance, interconnect heating, fuse coordination, fault propagation, aging, enclosure safety, charger safety, regulatory compliance, or permission to fabricate, charge, or energize a pack.

Those require separate evidence and later validated slices. Physical battery work remains outside automatic approval.
