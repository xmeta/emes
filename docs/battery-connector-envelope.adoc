= Pack-Side Connector Envelope

== Purpose

`tools/battery_connector_envelope.py` composes explicit pack-side connector limits with the existing weakest-known battery-component evidence.

It intentionally does not reopen or recompute the upstream cell/BMS/converter/fuse analysis. Instead it consumes the digest-bound parent evidence and adds connector candidates in their native electrical domains.

[source,text]
----
mechanism + power evidence + known-component evidence
                  |
                  v
       pack-side connector ratings
                  |
                  v
       minimum load-scale factor
                  |
                  v
        connector/path evidence
----

== Anderson SB50 reference assembly

The first real connector snapshot is an Anderson Power Products SB50 wire-to-wire mated assembly built from blue `992G4` housings and `5900` high-force contacts.

The reviewed manufacturer SB50 material reports the IEC configuration as:

* current rating AC/DC: 50 A;
* steady-state voltage rating AC/DC: 250 V;
* tested wire cross-section: 16 mm^2;
* tested contact series: 5900/1307.

The catalog record represents that reviewed _assembly configuration_. EMES does not attach the electrical rating to a bare housing in isolation, and it deliberately does not substitute the higher UL wire-to-wire rating for the IEC value used here.

The source snapshot is `catalogs/sources/anderson-sb50-992g4-5900.source.json`, normalized deterministically by `tools/providers/anderson_sb50.py` into `catalogs/anderson-sb50-992g4-5900.catalog.json`.

The current environment could read the official manufacturer PDF through the research browser but could not retrieve stable raw PDF bytes for local hashing. Therefore `raw_bytes_not_pinned` is explicit and no source digest is fabricated.

== Native-domain composition

For each declared `pack_connector_components` entry, the adapter computes:

[stem]
+++++
s_{connector} = \frac{I_{rated}}{I_{pack,max}}
+++++

and independently checks:

[stem]
+++++
V_{margin} = V_{connector,rated} - V_{pack,max}
+++++

A negative voltage margin is rejected. Current and voltage are not multiplied together to invent a connector power rating.

The parent known-component scale is retained as another candidate, so the final known path scale is:

[stem]
+++++
s_{path} = \min(s_{parent}, s_{connector,1}, \ldots)
+++++

== Reference result

For `examples/power-pack-molicel-p45b-connected/mechanism.json` at the existing 500 W load:

* nominal-point pack-side current is approximately 14.62 A, while the maximum over the modeled 25--42 V pack range is approximately 21.05 A;
* SB50 rated current is 50 A, giving a 2.375 load-scale factor against that maximum current;
* maximum pack voltage is 42 V;
* SB50 steady-state rating is 250 V, giving a 208 V margin;
* the upstream known-component envelope is 1.9, set by the SynQor 40 A input-current limit and tied by the nominal 40 A fuse;
* the composed known path output envelope is therefore 950 W.

== Counterexamples

Focused CI verifies that the connector actually participates in the decision:

* replacing the connector rating with 10 A moves the limiting candidate to `connector_rated_current:main_connector`, giving a 0.475 load scale and 237.5 W output envelope;
* replacing the connector voltage rating with 30 V is rejected against the 42 V maximum pack voltage;
* tampering with the parent known-envelope evidence linkage is rejected rather than accepted as a valid evidence chain.

== Remaining boundary

This slice does not model conductor thermal ampacity, cable bundling, ambient-temperature derating, crimp process capability, contact aging, contamination, enclosure heating, or contactor limits.

In particular, the manufacturer statement that a connector configuration was tested with 16 mm^2 wire is not treated as proof that every 16 mm^2 conductor installation is safe at the connector current rating. Conductor sizing remains a separate evidence problem.

Physical fabrication, charging, and energization remain outside automatic approval.
